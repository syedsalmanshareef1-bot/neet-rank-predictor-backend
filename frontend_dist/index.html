How this works

The predictor doesn't guess — it looks up what actually happened.

1. You enter a rank

Along with your home state, category and the course and counselling state you're interested in.

2. It checks eligibility, then real records

Seats reserved for another state's domicile candidates, or for categories you don't belong to, are removed first. Your rank is then compared with the round-wise closing ranks the counselling authorities published.

3. You see what cleared

Each college and course appears once, with the best seat route for you, the latest year's round-by-round closing ranks, and a chance label based on that latest year.

Understanding your result

A quick reference for reading NEET counselling outcomes.

Closing rank

The rank of the last candidate admitted to a specific college, course, category and quota in a given round. Clearing it doesn't guarantee a seat next year — cutoffs move year to year.

Category & quota

Cutoffs differ sharply by category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwD) and quota (All-India, State, Management, NRI). Always check results under your own category and quota, not just "General".

Rounds change things

Cutoffs usually loosen in later rounds (R2, R3, mop-up) as seats vacated by upgrades and withdrawals open up. A rank that misses Round 1 may still clear a later round.

Predictions are generated from historical closing-rank data and are indicative only — actual cutoffs vary by year and are set solely by the official counselling authorities (MCC / state authorities).
