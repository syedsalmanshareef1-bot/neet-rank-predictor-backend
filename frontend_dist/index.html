How this works

The predictor doesn't guess — it looks up what actually happened.

1. You enter a rank

Optionally narrow it down by exam, state, authority, course, category or quota.

2. It checks real records

Your rank is compared against historical round-wise closing ranks stored in the backend database — the same data the counselling authorities publish.

3. You see what cleared

Every college and course combination your rank would have cleared is shown, closest cutoffs first, each labelled by how comfortable the margin was.

Understanding your result

A quick reference for reading NEET counselling outcomes.

Closing rank

The rank of the last candidate admitted to a specific college, course, category and quota in a given round. Clearing it doesn't guarantee a seat next year — cutoffs move year to year.

Category & quota

Cutoffs differ sharply by category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwD) and quota (All-India, State, Management, NRI). Always check results under your own category and quota, not just "General".

Rounds change things

Cutoffs usually loosen in later rounds (R2, R3, mop-up) as seats vacated by upgrades and withdrawals open up. A rank that misses Round 1 may still clear a later round.

Predictions are generated from historical closing-rank data and are indicative only — actual cutoffs vary by year and are set solely by the official counselling authorities (MCC / state authorities). This tool does not conduct counselling, allot seats, or accept payments, and is not affiliated with any government body.
